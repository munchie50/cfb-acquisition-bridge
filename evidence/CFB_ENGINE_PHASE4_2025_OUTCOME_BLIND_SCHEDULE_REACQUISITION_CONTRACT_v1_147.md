## Purpose
Recover exact pregame metadata needed to construct Challenger-A 2025 holdout predictions without allowing target outcomes into prediction construction.

## Source/provenance
Fresh reacquisition must use a named, reproducible schedule source with retrieval timestamp and raw-byte/content hash. Prefer the same SportsDataverse/cfbfastR schedule lineage recorded by v1.92 when executable. Any different source is a new source and must not be labeled equivalent without set/field reconciliation.

Historical authority comparison: v1.92 recorded 762 unique admitted 2025 regular FBS-vs-FBS game IDs before explicit championship scope, with 9 explicitly championship-labeled and 753 scope-ready primary if explicit labels sufficient.

## Raw-vs-projected separation
Raw reacquired schedule may contain outcomes because the season is historical, but raw bytes are quarantine/source evidence only.

Prediction machinery may receive ONLY an outcome-blind projection with:
- season
- game_id
- start_date
- home_team
- away_team
- neutral_site
- population/scope metadata required to reproduce the frozen population rule
- source/provenance identifier

Projection MUST exclude or make inaccessible:
- home_points / away_points / score / winner
- postgame status/results
- betting market fields
- any derived outcome field

The projection must be generated by an isolated step and independently audited for forbidden columns before feature construction.

## Population qualification
Before feature construction:
1. unique nonmissing game_id;
2. parseable kickoff chronology;
3. nonmissing home/away identity;
4. deterministic neutral-site parsing;
5. reproduce or explicitly explain any difference from historical 762 admitted IDs;
6. preserve explicit championship classification separately;
7. do not change population based on model performance or outcomes.

A mismatch to 762 is a blocker requiring source/population reconciliation, not permission to tune scope.

## Temporal rule
For a target game G, any 2025 same-season cumulative feature may use only qualified games for that team with kickoff strictly earlier than G. Same-kickoff ambiguity fails closed unless authoritative ordering proves availability. G's own PBP and every later game's PBP are forbidden inputs.

## Freeze sequence
raw source hash -> outcome-blind schedule projection hash -> population reconciliation -> chronology-safe feature build -> independent leakage audit -> fair prediction generation -> prediction artifact hash/readback -> separate authorization before outcomes are joined/scored.

This contract authorizes schedule reacquisition/preparation only. It does not authorize 2025 outcome scoring.
