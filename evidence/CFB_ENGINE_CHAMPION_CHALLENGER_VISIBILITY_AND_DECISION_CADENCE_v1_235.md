# CFB Engine - Champion/Challenger Visibility and Decision Cadence v1.235

Status: ACTIVE OPERATIONAL / REVIEW CONTROL
Date: 2026-09-26
Parents: v1.229, v1.231, v1.233

## Purpose
Make Champion-versus-Challenger development status prominent and decision-useful without turning promotion into a last-minute or artificially slow process.

## Principle
Champion/Challenger comparison is a continuous development track, not a decision deferred until the final minutes before kickoff. The system should surface what is known early, update it when material evidence changes, and distinguish an early actionable view from a later confirmation or exception.

This does not weaken prospective controls, independent evaluation, or promotion authority. It changes visibility and cadence, not scientific standards.

## Weekly review prominence
Every Weekly CFB Beta Review must contain a prominent Champion vs Challenger Status section near the front. It must state:
- current Beta Champion identity and maturity state;
- active Challenger/candidate identity and parent lineage;
- what the Challenger is trying to improve;
- evidence accumulated so far, including positive, negative and inconclusive findings;
- prospective Beta sample/evidence accumulated where applicable;
- material areas where Champion and Challenger agree and disagree;
- unresolved blockers or evidence still required;
- current disposition: continue testing, revise hypothesis, retire candidate, qualified for further Beta, promotion candidate awaiting authorization, or other governed state;
- what specifically could change that disposition next.

Do not manufacture a winner, score, or promotion date merely to create certainty.

## Decision cadence
For game/betting operations, do not intentionally wait until the last possible moment to expose a useful decision. When sufficient governed information exists, surface an early state such as BET EARLY, BET NOW, WAIT, PASS, or INCONCLUSIVE with its timestamp, rationale, minimum acceptable number and known uncertainty. Later checks are confirmations or updates driven by material new information, not a reason to suppress the earlier view.

WAIT must have a reason and, where practical, a next trigger: expected market movement, injury/QB information, weather, source refresh, price threshold, or other identified dependency. Do not use WAIT as a default that drifts to kickoff.

If no material dependency remains and the evidence supports an actionable state, make that state visible. If a late change materially alters the downstream betting decision, append the new decision with provenance; never rewrite the earlier one.

## Challenger cadence
Challenger evaluation should proceed as evidence becomes available rather than accumulating indefinitely by default. Sunday QA must review whether each active Challenger has enough evidence for its next governed step, whether additional waiting is informative, and whether a test can be accelerated safely through better experiment design, independent evaluation, or prospective exposure.

Time itself is not evidence. Do not prolong Challenger status merely to satisfy an arbitrary duration. Conversely, do not shorten required prospective evidence merely to promote quickly.

## Scientific boundaries
Champion remains frozen until a governed replacement earns promotion. Challenger/Sandbox cannot contaminate Champion predictions or live decisions unless separately authorized for a defined comparison/test. Sandbox success alone is insufficient. Real-world prospective Beta evidence and independent evaluation remain required where applicable.

Scientific effect: none by itself. This control governs visibility, cadence and review prominence; it does not alter coefficients, features, predictions, calibration or promotion criteria.
