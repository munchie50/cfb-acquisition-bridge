# CFB Engine - Beta Learning Closure Contract v1.231

Status: ACTIVE
Date: 2026-09-26

Purpose: require closed-loop learning from controlled real-world Beta operation while preserving frozen Champion integrity.

Core loop: observe -> preserve -> classify -> diagnose -> disposition -> test -> prospective Beta exposure -> independent evaluation -> close -> retain lesson.

Every material Beta observation must receive a durable disposition and remain visible until closed or superseded. Relevant observations include prediction misses or strengths, exclusions, data/source defects, chronology issues, operational friction, market disagreements, decision/timing/execution issues, and control successes or failures.

Maintain an append-only logical lesson ledger in existing evidence/audit state. Where applicable retain event ID, timestamp and knowledge cutoff, affected game/workflow, Champion lineage, evidence, factual observation, classification, impact, control response, suspected dependency, uncertainty, disposition, linked hypothesis or repair, test evidence, closure status, and supersession.

Permitted dispositions include closed/no action, monitor, data-source repair candidate, operations repair candidate, control repair candidate, Challenger hypothesis, betting-layer hypothesis, and authorization escalation.

Sunday QA is the principal reconciliation point. It must reconcile new observations, review unresolved prior items, identify recurring patterns, preserve negative experiments, verify accepted repairs by readback, and state the evidence still required for open loops.

Model, feature, and betting-rule hypotheses go to Challenger/Sandbox. Sandbox success establishes controlled viability only. It does not establish real-world validity. A candidate intended to affect Beta Production must be frozen and earn governed prospective Beta evidence, independent evaluation, and applicable authorization.

Keep learning layers separate: football prediction; source/data; operations; market signal; selection/threshold; timing; execution; closing-market evidence; later-authorized portfolio/staking; and outcome/variance. Do not automatically attribute failure in one layer to another.

Learning aggressively does not mean changing aggressively. Preserve temporal integrity, frozen predictions and decisions, negative findings, and Champion/Sandbox separation. Never manufacture earlier evidence using later knowledge.

Beta maturity is not earned by elapsed time, wins, or Sandbox success. A future maturity checkpoint requires explicitly frozen criteria and authorization supported by sufficient prospective evidence.

No new scheduler is created. Existing CFB tasks remain the operational clock.

Scientific effect: none. This contract changes no Champion coefficients, features, scaling, predictions, exclusions, or betting decisions.
