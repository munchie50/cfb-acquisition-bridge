# CFB Engine — Beta Production Maturity Classification v1.229

Status: **ACTIVE MATURITY / TERMINOLOGY AUTHORITY**
Date: 2026-09-26
Parents: v1.222, v1.228

## Classification

The current working CFB Champion is the **CFB Beta Production Champion**.

"Production" describes operational role: the model is the active working Champion used in the controlled real-world CFB workflow.

"Beta" describes maturity: this is an operational model undergoing controlled prospective real-world use, evidence collection, market comparison, execution observation, outcome evaluation, and continued governance validation. Production status must not be interpreted as a claim that the system is fully mature, fully validated across all betting concepts, or ready for uncontrolled deployment.

## Historical distinction

The former production-v1 state is retired and remains a non-operational historical reference.

Its historical "production" label represented the prior engine/framework state. It must not be used to imply that it was equivalent in operational maturity or functionality to the current working Beta Production Champion.

The current Champion originated as Challenger B and became the first working model placed into the controlled operational Champion role under v1.222. This maturity clarification does not create a statistical superiority claim over the retired legacy state.

## Controlled-beta purpose

The current phase is intended to:
- operate the working Champion prospectively in real-world conditions;
- preserve independent pre-event predictions and lineage;
- compare frozen Champion opinions with separately qualified market information;
- observe betting decisions, timing, execution and closing-market evidence separately;
- score outcomes without rewriting pre-event state;
- expose operational weaknesses and missing controls;
- generate governed evidence for future Champion and betting-layer development.

## Boundaries

Beta does not mean experimental freedom inside production.

All existing controls remain binding:
- frozen Champion model identity;
- FIRST_FROZEN immutability and REFRESH_SNAPSHOT lineage;
- no outcome-driven refit/recalibration/redesign;
- market/model separation;
- screenshot ingestion boundary;
- source/temporal qualification;
- betting evidence versus calibrated betting-model distinction;
- Champion/Challenger isolation;
- fail-closed behavior;
- explicit authorization gates where applicable.

Challenger/sandbox remains the location for experimental model or betting-rule development. Beta Production Champion remains the controlled operational reference.

## Terminology

Preferred current terms:
- **CFB Beta Production Champion**
- **Beta Champion** when context is unambiguous
- **Champion** as shorthand where maturity is not at issue

Historical evidence keeps its original terminology for audit integrity.

## Future maturity transition

Removal of the Beta classification requires a later explicit maturity checkpoint supported by accumulated prospective operational evidence and whatever acceptance criteria are frozen for that transition. It does not occur automatically because the model has been used for a period of time or because individual wagers/results are favorable.

## Scientific effect

None. This is a maturity/role clarification only. It changes no coefficients, features, scaling, predictions, exclusions, market observations, decisions, outcomes, or historical evidence.
